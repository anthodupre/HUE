      Trouble connecting?
      • Use Chrome or Edge, with Bluetooth turned on.
      • Close the Hue app on your phone. The light accepts one connection at a time.
      • A new computer must be paired once: open Pairing steps.
      • Schedules and timers run only while Iris is open and the computer is awake.

      Pair the light once, then it works

      The light accepted the connection but refuses commands, because it doesn’t trust this computer yet. Hue lights only accept a new device while they are discoverable.

        Reconnect by itself

        For Iris to reconnect to your lamp when it opens, Chrome needs one setting turned on. You only do this once.

        1. Copy this address, paste it in a new tab’s address bar, and press Enter:
        2. Next to “Use the new permissions backend for Web Bluetooth”, choose Enabled.
        3. Click Relaunch at the bottom of the page. Your tabs and apps come back.
        4. Open Iris and click Connect one last time. From then on, it reconnects when it opens.
