    This browser can’t use Bluetooth. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer with Bluetooth turned on.

    Trouble connecting?
    • Use Chrome or Edge on your computer, with Bluetooth turned on.
    • Close the Hue app on your phone. The light may accept only one connection at a time.
    • Before connecting from a new computer or phone, make the light discoverable from the Hue app (Settings → Voice assistants → Amazon Alexa or Google Home → Make discoverable). The light only accepts a new device for a few minutes after that.
    • If it connects but nothing changes, run the test in Diagnostics. It tells you whether the light refuses the commands and how to fix it on your system.
    • On Windows, the light must be paired in Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device while it is discoverable, before you connect here.
    • Schedules and the timer run only while this page is open and your computer is awake.
